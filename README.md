# Blinkit Sales Analysis Using MySQL

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Blinkit sales data using MySQL to identify sales trends, top-performing product categories, outlet performance, and customer ratings.

## Objectives

* Analyze sales across different outlet types.
* Identify top-selling product categories.
* Compare sales across outlet location tiers.
* Evaluate outlet size and establishment year performance.
* Analyze sales by product fat content.
* Explore customer ratings across product categories.
* Identify top-performing products within each category.
* Perform data quality checks.

## Tools & Technologies

* MySQL
* SQL

## SQL Concepts Used

* SELECT, WHERE, ORDER BY
* Aggregate functions: SUM(), AVG(), COUNT()
* GROUP BY and HAVING
* CASE WHEN statements
* NULL value checks
* Subqueries
* Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
* Window functions: ROW_NUMBER()
* Data standardization

## Key Business Insights

**1. Outlet Performance:**
Supermarket Type 1 generates the highest total sales among the analyzed outlet types, at 787,549.89.

**2. Product Categories:**
Fruits and Vegetables lead with sales of 147,188.96, followed by Snack Foods at 144,949.13.

**3. Outlet Location:**
After standardizing location tier labels, Tier 2 outlets record the highest total sales at 393,150.65.

**4. Outlet Size:**
Medium-sized outlets generate 377,181.06 in sales, slightly ahead of Small outlets at 370,986.59.

**5. Product Fat Content:**
Low Fat products contribute 644,516.74 in sales, compared with 352,642.49 for Regular products.

**6. Customer Ratings:**
Meat records the highest average rating at 4.01 among the reported product categories.

#Business Recommendations

* Monitor leading product categories to support sales growth.
* Evaluate the operational practices of high-performing outlet types.
* Compare sales and operating costs before making outlet expansion decisions.
* Standardize inconsistent labels to improve reporting accuracy.
* Monitor customer ratings to identify opportunities to improve customer satisfaction.
* Use product-level analysis to support inventory and merchandising decisions.
